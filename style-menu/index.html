Words you might hear

Captions
The words of what you say, shown on screen. Most people watch with the sound off, so captions keep them with you.
Kinetic captions
Captions that move: words pop or light up as you say them. That's caption styles 3 and 4.
Hook or title
The line at the top in the first two or three seconds. It tells people why to stay.
Paper cut
Text on torn-paper pieces, like a collage. Caption 5 and title 3.
Cover
The still picture people see on your profile grid. Unrushed Edit makes one for every Reel in your title style.
Font mood
A pair of typefaces that set the feeling: one for titles, one for captions.
B-roll
Cutting to your painting while your voice keeps going. It hides the little jumps where pauses were cut. (Cinematic Studio)
Punch-in
Gently moving closer on some sentences, like a film camera. (Cinematic Studio)
Film look
The colour and mood of the picture, like warm golden light or soft black and white. (Cinematic Studio)
Design style
The whole look of a post or page: shapes, textures, colours and mood. Wabi-sabi, Gzhel and Cozy blanket sit most naturally beside hand-painted work.

The tall phone shape Reels use. Your footage is fitted to it automatically.
