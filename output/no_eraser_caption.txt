I stopped using an eraser. Here's why ✒️

1. Most beginners don't start because they fear what they make won't be good. Leaving the eraser behind is the assurance, from the very beginning, that this page is meant to be imperfect.

2. For anxious people, an eraser creates more anxiety. When you have the option to erase, you will choose to perfect, so your hand keeps reaching for it. Without it you have to face the mess instead of correcting it, and eventually that makes you calmer.

3. Life gives us the option to improvise or to redo, never to undo. You never stop living because of that. Why should art be any different?
