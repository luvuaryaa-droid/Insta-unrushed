INSTAGRAM CAPTION

I stopped using an eraser. Here's why ✒️

1️⃣ Most beginners don't start because they're afraid it won't be good. Leaving the eraser behind is a promise, right from the first line, that this page is meant to be imperfect.

2️⃣ For anxious minds, an eraser creates more anxiety. If you can erase, you'll keep chasing perfect. Without it, you have to face the mess, and slowly that makes you calmer.

3️⃣ Life lets us improvise or redo, but never undo. We don't stop living because of that. Why should art be any different?

Your turn: one page, in pen, no eraser 🖊️
