Painting My Way Back to Myself: Day 1 🌺

A self portrait. It started with a single line and one red petal, then a hibiscus bloomed across half my face, and I finished it tile by tile.

Somewhere in the middle I stopped worrying about getting it right and just kept going. This series is me showing up for myself, one painting at a time. See you tomorrow ✨
