From pencil to diya 🪔

One little Deepawali card, 14 photos, no rushing. It started as a pencil sketch, then a wash of deep blue, then colour by colour: the diya, the flowers, the leaves, two golden fish and a lotus. Ink outlines, dots of light and a striped border came last.

My favourite step? Watching the flame finally glow. Which one is yours? 👇

Shubh Deepawali 💛 May your home be full of light.
