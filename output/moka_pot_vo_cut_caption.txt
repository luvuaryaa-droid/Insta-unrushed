You get better by walking the path, not before it. 🤍

For years I kept my sketchbook closed. I studied finance, never art, so I figured my drawings weren't "ready" for anyone to see.

This moka pot took me days. Look at frame 1A: wobbly outlines, an empty mug, a spoon that's just a shape. That stage is real art too.

Being good isn't what brings the joy back. Letting yourself be seen while you're learning does.

Creating is a journey.
Waiting until your art is "worth sharing" means leaving out the main story.
Without the mess, the challenge, the fear and the overcoming, the becoming means nothing.
