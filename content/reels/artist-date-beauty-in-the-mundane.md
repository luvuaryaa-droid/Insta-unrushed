## Caption

Today's artist date had no plan. Just one rule: notice 5 beautiful, boring things.

A bird stretching its wings.
Flowers nobody planted for me.
A colour I couldn't stop looking at.
A path I didn't need to rush.
The city, still going, while I slowed down.

When my mind gets loud, noticing small things is one of the few ways I come back to my body. Julia Cameron writes that the reward for attention is always healing. I didn't fully understand that until I tried it.

Your turn: on your next walk, find 5 ordinary things that are quietly beautiful. No photos needed. Just notice.
