# Hook Bank: Gentle Art for Anxious Women

100 hooks for @the_unrushed.whispers, plus 30 topics that combine The Artist's Way with gentle art for anxious women.

**How to use a hook**
- Put it on screen in the first second, over your art or footage (never a title card or blank frame).
- Say it out loud too if it's a talking head.
- Keep text in the middle of the frame, away from the top 15% and bottom 25% (Instagram covers those areas).
- Fill in the blanks (___) with your real detail. Specific always beats general.
- Never promise to cure anxiety. Talk about calming, slowing down, feeling safer.

Format key: **[P]** art process (hands), **[T]** talking head, **[D]** artist date footage
